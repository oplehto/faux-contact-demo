The CRT raster

A narrow band rolls down the tube. Within it, the grid bows sideways and the sprite bends by the same amount. The rows settle back into register as the band passes.

The title cut

Give each chapter one word, one type treatment and one field of colour. Hold it until the reader advances.

The cartridge

Choose a pixel budget before drawing. This coupling fits a 192 × 152 sprite in seven inks. Two shifted bands and an ordered dither give the rounded metal a chipped edge.

Display conditions
Clean
One 64 px tracking band travels from above the screen to below it every seven seconds. The picture shifts up to 8 px sideways inside the band.
Worn
Add fixed dust and offset two thin slices of the sprite by two or three source pixels. Give the dust the same position on every frame.
03

Use the sheets

Load signal.css, then screen.css. Add fc to a wrapper and put your content above the decorative layers. Add type-fx.css for an RGB-split display title and film.css for amber film stock. Load js/crt.js with defer to roll the entire picture. All assets work offline.

Set data-wear to clean or worn. The sprite slip comes from swapping the two supplied PNGs. Use the same technique with your own artwork.

Set data-motion="paused" on the screen to freeze the current frame. Reduced motion gives you a straight, steady raster.

Grid controls
--fc-grid
Ink for the horizontal and vertical traces.
--fc-cell
Grid cell width and height.
--fc-band
Band height in pixels. Start at 64 px.
--fc-shift
Peak horizontal deflection in pixels. Start at 8 px.

Time for one top-to-bottom pass. Start at 7 seconds.
